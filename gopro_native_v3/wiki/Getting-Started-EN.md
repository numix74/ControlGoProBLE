# Getting Started

## What you need

- An **Android 8.0+** phone with Bluetooth enabled
- A **GoPro HERO 9, 10, 11 or 12** (charged, powered on)
- The **AirBubble** app installed

---

## Step 1 — Prepare your GoPro

1. Power on your GoPro
2. Make sure **Bluetooth is enabled** on the camera
   _(Settings → Connections → Bluetooth → On)_
3. You do **not need to pair** from your phone's Bluetooth settings — AirBubble handles everything automatically

---

## Step 2 — Install AirBubble

> _APK / Play Store link coming soon_

For now, install from the provided APK:
1. Download the `.apk` file
2. On your phone: **Settings → Security → Unknown sources** (allow)
3. Open the `.apk` file and install

---

## Step 3 — Connect

1. Open **AirBubble**
2. Grant the requested permissions _(Bluetooth, Location)_
3. Tap **CONNECT**
4. The app scans and connects automatically to the first GoPro it finds
5. Once connected, you land on the **Dashboard**

> 💡 If the connection fails: check that Bluetooth is on for both devices, and that the GoPro is within 10 metres.

---

## Step 4 — Your first recording

On the **Dashboard**:

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **START CAPTURE** | Start recording |
| **STOP CAPTURE** | Stop recording |
| **HiLight** | Tag a moment _during_ recording |
| **Sleep** | Put the camera to sleep |

---

## Step 5 — Enable the floating bubble

1. Go to **Settings** (bottom tab)
2. Enable **Floating Bubble**
3. Allow "Display over other apps" if Android asks
4. The bubble appears — you can now close AirBubble and control your camera from anywhere

→ [Learn more about the floating bubble](Floating-Bubble-EN)

---

## Auto-reconnect

If you move away from the camera and lose signal, AirBubble attempts to **reconnect automatically** up to 3 times (2s, 4s, 8s intervals).

> Auto-reconnect is **disabled** if you deliberately press **Disconnect** or **Sleep** — this is intentional.

---

## Common first-start issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| CONNECT button stays greyed out | Phone Bluetooth is disabled |
| Scan runs but finds nothing | GoPro is off or GoPro Bluetooth is disabled |
| Connection fails repeatedly | Restart the GoPro and try scanning again |
| Permission denied | Go to Settings → Apps → AirBubble → Permissions |
